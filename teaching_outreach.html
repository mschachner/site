Teaching

I love to teach! In spring 2026, I am an adjunct faculty member at Cayuga Community College, teaching MATH 112 Contemporary Mathematics for the the Cornell Prison Education Program. I previously taught for the program in spring 2025.

I received a Graduate Student Teaching Award from the Cornell mathematics department in December 2025.

In addition, I have four semesters of experience teaching at Cornell:

  • in spring 2024, I was instructor of record for MATH 1120 Calculus II;
  • in autumn 2023, I was head TA / recitation TA for MATH 2940 Linear Algebra for Engineers;
  • in spring 2023, I was a recitation teaching assistant for MATH 2210 Linear Algebra; and
  • in autumn 2022, I was a recitation teaching assistant for MATH 1910 Calculus II for Engineers.

Outreach

I'm active in Cornell's chapter of the Association for Women in Mathematics, and currently serve on the outreach team. I previously served on the professional development team in spring 2024.

In spring 2026, autumn 2025, spring 2025, autumn 2024, spring 2024, and autumn 2022, I have been a mentor to multiple undergraduate students as part of the Cornell mathematics directed reading program.

In the summers of 2019 and 2020, I was a teaching assistant/counselor for BEAM, an outreach program which helps increase the accessibility of advanced mathematics for underserved students.

In the spring of 2023, I was an instructor for Little Circle, a Cornell-run outreach program for students in grades 3-5. We learned all about infinity, fractals, and more.
