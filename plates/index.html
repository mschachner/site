How to play

Try to make as many words as you can out of the letters in the plate! All the letters in the plate must show up in order in your word, but they don't need to be next to each other. For the clue O-G-E, FORGET and ORANGE are valid, but GEODE is not valid, because the G comes before the O.

Scoring

  • Length: 5 points for every letter beyond three.
  • Buried: +25 if the word neither starts with the plate's first letter nor ends with its last, +10 if it's buried at one end only.
  • Snug: +15 if the three letters appear consecutively, as in TOGETHER.
  • Vanity Plate: one special word on every plate is worth +250.

Hints are available, use as many as you need!
