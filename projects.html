Leaper

Open Leaper

A toy project of mine is to study the notion of a hop on a graph. This is a permutation of the vertices in which every element "hops" to an adjacent element. The hops generate a subgroup of the symmetric group on the vertices, called the leap group of the graph.


In autumn 2023 and spring 2024, I and several others in the Cornell math department studied leap groups. A project with our results is in preparation. Recently, I've been building Leaper, a tool for exploring the hops and leaps of various graphs.

Milestone

Open Milestone

I made an abstract strategy game called Milestone, which became the subject of a computer science project by Connor McCarthy, Corban Chiu, and Sunny Chavan. They built a tree-search AI using an evaluation powered by hyper-heuristic tuning, and trained a neural network as well which played nearly half a million games! You can find their report here.
